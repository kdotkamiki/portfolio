About

I help brands turn cultural insight, platform fluency and strong creative ideas into social campaigns that travel, across B2B and B2C.

I've spent 15+ years on both sides of the feed. Twelve as a creator building my own audience; the rest leading social for Riot Games, G2 Esports, Edelman and Google Cloud. That mix means I know what a brief needs to say and what a post needs to feel like.

Today I lead social content for Google Cloud, turning dense technology stories into work that senior, technical and general audiences actually stop for, and run an executive programme that helps Google leaders show up on social as themselves.
