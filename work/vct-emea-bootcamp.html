13

Paid and organic sign-up campaign

VCT EMEA Bootcamp

Riot Games · In-house

Client
Riot Games
Employer
Riot Games (in-house)
Year
2023
Platforms
X, Instagram, YouTube · paid social

Overview

A personalised fan experience that turned each player's own VALORANT data into a bespoke Bootcamp video and matched them with a VCT EMEA pro, driven by a joint social, media and paid campaign.

The challenge

The goal was simple and measurable: get as many people as possible to sign up for their bespoke Bootcamp data, in a feed already crowded with esports content.

My role

As Social Media Strategist, EMEA at Riot Games, I owned the social and creative strategy for VALORANT Esports in the region, connecting audience insight with global brand objectives and taking campaigns from brief to launch to measurement.

On Bootcamp my role was creative input and social campaign planning: shaping how the experience would be presented on social, building the organic rollout and reminders, and working jointly with the media and paid teams so organic and paid pushed toward one sign-up goal.

Strategic approach

A personalisation-led acquisition campaign:

  1. Make the fan the heroEach person gets a video built from their own data, which is worth sharing.
  2. Give them a pro to compare withMatching fans with a VCT EMEA pro links the experience back to the league.
  3. Run organic and paid as oneJoint planning with media and paid, with every touchpoint driving to the sign-up page.
  4. Use urgencyLast-chance reminders before the experience closed.

Creative idea

VCT EMEA Bootcamp: create your personalised video and compare yourself with the pros.

What I delivered

  1. Social campaign plan and organic rollout
  2. Creative input on the experience and launch assets
  3. Joint campaign with media and paid
  4. Reminder posts ahead of close

Results

80K+

Sign-ups for a bespoke Bootcamp video

23.4K

Views on a single last-chance reminder post on X
