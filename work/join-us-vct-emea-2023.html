League launch

Join Us: VCT EMEA 2023

Riot Games · In-house

Client
Riot Games
Employer
Riot Games (in-house)
Year
2023
Platforms
YouTube, X, Instagram, TikTok

Overview

The launch films for the 2023 season of VCT EMEA, the first year of VALORANT's partnership league: Join Us, and the opening teaser.

The challenge

2023 was a new structure for VALORANT esports in EMEA, with ten partner teams and a new studio in Berlin. Fans needed a reason to commit to a league they hadn't seen yet.

My role

As Social Media Strategist, EMEA at Riot Games, I owned the social and creative strategy for VALORANT Esports in the region, connecting audience insight with global brand objectives and taking campaigns from brief to launch to measurement.

For the launch, I set the social and creative strategy, worked with the creative and production teams on the concept and shoot, planned the content calendar and rollout across channels, and owned performance tracking after launch.

Strategic approach

A people-first launch campaign:

  1. Lead with the playersPersonalities sell a new league faster than logos and formats do.
  2. Make it localPut the players in ordinary places around Berlin, the league's new home.
  3. Sequence the launchThe Join Us film to invite fans in, then a cinematic opening teaser on the eve of the season.
  4. Extend the shootPhotography from the same shoot to feed launch-week posts and always-on content.

Creative idea

Join Us: players in everyday Berlin, each with a character beat, inviting fans into the new league rather than announcing it at them.

What I delivered

  1. Join Us launch film
  2. Photography from the Join Us shoot
  3. VCT EMEA 2023 opening teaser
