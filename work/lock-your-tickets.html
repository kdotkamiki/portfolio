12

Paid ticketing campaign

Lock Your Tickets

Riot Games · In-house

Client
Riot Games
Employer
Riot Games (in-house)
Year
2023
Platforms
X, YouTube, Instagram · paid social

Overview

A paid and organic campaign to sell tickets for VCT EMEA's new studio, built from the ground up at Messe Berlin in 2023.

The challenge

A brand-new venue with no track record, and an audience used to watching online. The campaign had to turn viewers into ticket buyers, and prove it.

My role

As Social Media Strategist, EMEA at Riot Games, I owned the social and creative strategy for VALORANT Esports in the region, connecting audience insight with global brand objectives and taking campaigns from brief to launch to measurement.

On Lock Your Tickets I owned the social campaign: the creative brief for the trailer, the organic rollout, and the paid set-up with our media partners, with UTM tracking on every link so ticket clicks and purchases could be attributed back to the creative.

Strategic approach

A conversion-led, full-funnel ticketing campaign:

  1. Lead with hypeA trailer that makes the live room feel like the place the season happens.
  2. Put paid behind itPaid social to reach in-market fans beyond the organic audience.
  3. Track every clickUTMs on all links, so performance is read against ticket demand, not just views.
  4. Optimise on conversionShift spend and messaging towards what was actually selling tickets.

Creative idea

Lock Your Tickets: a fast, red-drenched trailer that makes the live room feel like the place the season happens.

What I delivered

  1. Ticketing trailer
  2. Paid social campaign with UTM tracking
  3. Organic launch posts
