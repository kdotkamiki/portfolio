The challenge

Year two of a partnership league has a different job from year one. 2024 brought a new Kickoff tournament, a new team promoted through Ascension and a season split into stages: each chapter needed its own reason to tune in, and returning fans needed new reasons to stay between the tentpoles.

My role

As Social & Content Lead at Edelman on the Riot Games account, I owned the creative and content pipeline for VALORANT Esports across EMEA: shaping briefs, setting creative direction, steering production partners and reporting performance back to senior stakeholders across nine markets.

Strategic approach

Make every chapter an event, and keep what fans came back for:

  1. Find the story of the momentKickoff is about change, Stage 2 about a new look and new stakes, the Grand Finals about legacy.
  2. Give each chapter its own creative worldNew visual language and tone each time, built on the league's new 2024 brand.
  3. Refresh, don't repeatThe strongest 2023 formats returned with new mechanics, so returning fans got something new.
  4. Go deeper on playersDocumentary content, measured on sentiment and engagement rather than raw reach.
  5. Cut natively, then iterateBespoke edits for every platform; read reach, engagement and sentiment and carry the learnings into the next chapter.

Always-on content

The formats fans came back for, refreshed for a second season, and a new way to meet the players.

Player documentary series

Meet the Player

A series of mini-documentaries about players' lives in and outside VALORANT: the ups and downs of a career, told at home. It started with G2's Mimi in her hometown in Denmark. Documentary content builds affinity rather than raw reach, so sentiment and engagement were the KPIs.

My role

I helped select the players worth featuring, shaped the editorial angle for each film, supported the on-location production and set the measurement framework, putting sentiment and engagement ahead of reach.
