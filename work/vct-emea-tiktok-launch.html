06

Platform launch

VCT EMEA on TikTok

Riot Games · At Edelman

Client
Riot Games
Employer
Edelman
Year
2024
Platforms
TikTok

Overview

The launch of VCT EMEA's TikTok channel, built on native content captured with players and talent, which grew to 100,000 followers in its first year.

The challenge

A new channel starts at zero, and TikTok audiences scroll past anything that looks like a repurposed broadcast clip. Player time is limited across a season, so native content had to be planned rather than hoped for.

My role

As Social & Content Lead at Edelman on the Riot Games account, I owned the creative and content pipeline for VALORANT Esports across EMEA: shaping briefs, setting creative direction, steering production partners and reporting performance back to senior stakeholders across nine markets.

I owned the TikTok launch campaign end to end: the channel strategy and content pillars, the shot lists and formats for media day, on-site direction with players and talent, and the publishing cadence and optimisation through the first year.

Strategic approach

A platform-first launch playbook:

  1. Start nativeNo repurposed broadcast clips. Every launch asset is written for the For You page.
  2. Piggyback on accessUse media day, when evergreen content for the year is captured, to also capture TikTok-native shorts with players and talent.
  3. Build content pillarsTrends, personality and behind-the-scenes formats that can be repeated and scaled across the year.
  4. Test, learn, double downRead what the algorithm rewards and put more behind the formats that break out.

Creative idea

Content that works on the For You page first and feels true to the people in it: short, self-aware formats and trends performed by the players and talent themselves.

What I delivered

  1. TikTok launch campaign
  2. Native shorts planned and captured at media day
  3. Ongoing organic publishing through the first year
