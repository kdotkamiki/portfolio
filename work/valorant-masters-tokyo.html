09

International event campaign

VALORANT Masters Tokyo

Riot Games · In-house

Client
Riot Games
Employer
Riot Games (in-house)
Year
2023
Platforms
X, Instagram, YouTube

Overview

EMEA's social campaign for VALORANT Masters Tokyo 2023: bespoke anime-style artwork of the region's players, and event coverage written with Japanese VALORANT slang, built with Riot Games' Tokyo office.

The challenge

Masters Tokyo was the first VALORANT Masters held in Japan. EMEA's coverage had to rally fans behind the region's four teams, and land with Japanese fans welcoming the event on home soil, without reading like a translation.

My role

As Social Media Strategist, EMEA at Riot Games, I owned the social and creative strategy for VALORANT Esports in the region, connecting audience insight with global brand objectives and taking campaigns from brief to launch to measurement.

On Masters Tokyo I led EMEA's social campaign: the illustrated team assets, the localisation work with Riot Games' Tokyo office, and coverage across the event, from the Road to Masters film through to the winners' wallpaper for Fnatic. It's the campaign I'm proudest of from my time on VALORANT Esports.

Strategic approach

Local first, so it could travel globally:

  1. Partner with the local teamWe worked with Riot Games' Tokyo office so the campaign would land with Japanese audiences.
  2. Build a shared vocabularyTogether we compiled the phrases and slang the Japanese VALORANT community uses, each one approved by the Tokyo office.
  3. Write it into everythingThat language ran through all of our social copy during event coverage, so Japanese fans saw their own community in it.
  4. Give fans something to keepDesktop and mobile wallpapers of each team carried the event beyond match days.
  5. Stay on through the finalAlways-on match coverage through to the final, and a winners' wallpaper when Fnatic took the title.

Creative idea

Bespoke anime-style illustrations of EMEA's players at VALORANT Masters Tokyo: a visual language that belongs to the host city, carried through the event in several forms, from team artwork and wallpapers to match-day coverage.

What I delivered

  1. Road to Masters Tokyo hero film
  2. Anime-style artwork for Fnatic, Team Liquid, NAVI and FUT Esports
  3. Desktop and mobile wallpapers
  4. Always-on match coverage assets
  5. Japanese-localised social copy, approved with Riot Games' Tokyo office
  6. Winners' wallpaper for champions Fnatic

Results

1M

Impressions across static assets

200K+

Views across video

10K+

Engagements

Engagement and positive sentiment from Japanese fans rose sharply across X, YouTube community posts and Instagram. The campaign served the local community and the global one at the same time.
