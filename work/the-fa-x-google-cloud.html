The FA × Google Cloud

The FA · Google Cloud · At Jellyfish

Client
The FA · Google Cloud
Employer
Jellyfish
Year
2026
Platforms
Vertical social video (9:16)

Overview

A vertical video series that put England players in front of Google Cloud’s AI tools, so football fans could see what Gemini does by watching players compete with it.

The challenge

AI products are abstract, and sports partnership content often ends up as players standing next to a logo. This had to engage a football audience in a conversation about AI, and feel high-energy and playful rather than like a product demo.

My role

As Social Content Manager at Jellyfish, I lead social on the Google Cloud account.

On this partnership I was part of the core team behind the campaign, leading on Google Cloud’s social: pressure-testing the hero concepts for social, shaping the formats around what talent time allowed, and making sure every piece met the standard Google Cloud expects before it went live. I worked alongside the client, The FA and our production partners through production, edit and approvals.

Strategic approach

Product-led storytelling, disguised as play:

  1. Find the tensionFootball fans don’t care about AI features, but they love competition, banter and seeing players off-guard.
  2. Make the product the gameEach hero concept turns one Gemini capability into a challenge, so the tech is understood by watching it work, not by being told.
  3. Build a content systemTwo hero films carry the product story; lighter quick-fire formats deliver personality, frequency and cut-downs between them.
  4. Design for the feedVertical-first, hook in the opening seconds, split screens, stat cards, captions and sound design built for sound-off and sound-on viewing.
  5. One shoot, many assetsPlan the formats around a single block of player access to maximise output per minute of talent time.

Creative idea

AI RoboBall

Robot football powered by Gemini. Each robot starts with a basic Python program. The players improve it by telling Gemini how the robot should play, and Gemini Code Assist turns their instructions into code using its knowledge of the game and the robot’s API. A three-minute countdown, GoPros on the players for split-screen footage and stat cards for each player build the drama before kick-off.

Creative Agent drawing challenge

Players draw to a football theme, then the Creative Agent uses Veo and Imagen to turn each sketch into an image and a short video. Split screens capture the drawings and the reactions, and the players judge whose creation best fits the brief.

Quick-fire formats

Overrated or Underrated, and a Get to Know format of fast, culturally relevant questions, to show more of who the players are.

What I delivered

  1. AI RoboBall hero film
  2. Creative Agent drawing challenge hero film
  3. Overrated or Underrated
  4. Get to Know quick-fire
  5. A quick-fire numbers guessing game
