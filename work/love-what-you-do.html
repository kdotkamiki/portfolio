06

Player documentary series

Love What You Do

Riot Games · At Edelman

Client
Riot Games
Employer
Edelman
Year
2024
Platforms
YouTube, X, Instagram

Overview

A series of mini-documentaries about players' lives in and outside VALORANT, starting with G2's Mimi in her hometown in Denmark.

The challenge

Fans know players through their gameplay, not as people. Reach-led formats grow numbers but rarely build lasting fandom for individual players.

My role

Social & Content Lead on the Riot Games account at Edelman. We chose the players to feature deliberately, and I helped shape the series and its measurement.

Strategic approach

Choose a small number of players worth knowing better, go to where they're from, and judge the series on the right metric. Documentary pieces tend to engage deeply rather than reach widely, so sentiment and engagement were the KPIs, not views.

Creative idea

Love What You Do: the ups and downs of a career, inside the game and outside it, told at home. For Mimi, that meant filming in her hometown in Denmark.

What I delivered

  1. Four player feature films
  2. On-location shoot in Denmark for the Mimi episode
  3. Social cutdowns and distribution

Results

99%+

Positive sentiment

8,000+

Engagements across the four films
