B2B event campaign

London Summit 2026

Google Cloud · At Jellyfish

Client
Google Cloud
Employer
Jellyfish
Year
2026
Platforms
LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook

Overview

An earned and owned social campaign built to make Google Cloud's London Summit the UK's reference point for AI and data cloud, and to make the day's content keep working long after the keynote ended.

The challenge

The brief was explicit: establish Google Cloud as the leading authority in AI and data cloud in the UK market. Every major cloud provider was making that claim, to the same audience of technical and business decision-makers.

Event content usually peaks on the day and disappears. The challenge was to turn one summit into authority that showed up in owned channels, in people's own feeds, and in trade media.

My role

As Social Content Manager at Jellyfish and Social Content Lead on the Google Cloud account, I own content output end to end, from brief ingestion and creative direction through production, approvals and publishing, across Google Cloud and its partner portfolio.

On the Summit, I led the social content workstream: translating the mission statement into a channel plan, aligning the Google Cloud team, Jellyfish strategists and production partners on one content roadmap, briefing the on-site capture, and keeping executive, advocacy and brand content moving at the speed of a live event. After the day, I tracked performance against the KPIs for each layer and fed the learnings into the next activation.

Strategic approach

A full-funnel social ecosystem, with every layer given its own job and its own KPI:

  1. Define the jobOne mission: authority in AI and data cloud in the UK. Every asset had to ladder up to it.
  2. Owned builds authorityLinkedIn, X, Instagram and Facebook carry the brand narrative, measured on weighted engagement rather than vanity reach.
  3. Earned builds trustEmployee advocacy through Halo, creators and the executives in the Luminary Programme put real voices behind the message, measured on share of voice and reach.
  4. Paid builds scaleTechTarget and Condé Nast extend the story to in-market decision-makers, measured on impressions and sentiment.
  5. Capture once, publish everywhereOn-site capture designed for every layer at once, so one moment from the stage becomes a brand post, an executive update, an employee share and a paid placement.
  6. Read and optimiseLive monitoring on the day, then a full read-out against target to sharpen the next event.

Creative idea

Treat the Summit as a content studio, not just a stage. Executives, speakers and Gemini masterclass sessions were captured on the day as platform-native video and stories, then distributed through every layer above. The campaign's own line put it simply: don't just capture your event, scale it.

What I delivered

  1. Owned, earned and paid channel strategy with a KPI per layer
  2. Executive and speaker videos filmed at the Summit
  3. Live and on-the-day content across LinkedIn, X, Instagram and Facebook, including Instagram Stories
  4. Employee advocacy and executive amplification through Halo and the Luminary Programme
  5. Results reel summarising the campaign
