Always-on content

The 2024 Content Slate

Riot Games · At Edelman

Client
Riot Games
Employer
Edelman
Year
2024
Platforms
YouTube, X, Instagram, TikTok

Overview

The always-on content that carried VCT EMEA through its second year of partnership, building on the formats that worked in 2023.

The challenge

Year two of a partnership league has a different job from year one: hold on to the audience the first season built, and give returning fans new reasons to tune in between the tentpole moments.

My role

As Social & Content Lead at Edelman on the Riot Games account, I owned the creative and content pipeline for VALORANT Esports across EMEA: shaping briefs, setting creative direction, steering production partners and reporting performance back to senior stakeholders across nine markets.

Across the 2024 slate I carried the league's always-on formats into their second season, refreshing the series fans had come back for in 2023.

Strategic approach

Build on what year one proved:

  1. Keep what fans came back forThe strongest 2023 formats returned for a second season.
  2. Refresh, don't repeatNew episodes and new angles, so returning fans got something they hadn't seen.
  3. Keep the rhythmAlways-on content between the tentpoles, so the channels never went quiet.
  4. Learn from the dataUse what the 2023 numbers said to decide where to put more weight.
