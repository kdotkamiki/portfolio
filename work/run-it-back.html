Play-analysis series

Run It Back

Riot Games · In-house, then at Edelman

Client
Riot Games
Employer
Riot Games, then Edelman
Year
2023–24
Platforms
YouTube

Overview

A play-analysis series on the 2023 and 2024 content slates, with players from VCT EMEA and VCT Game Changers EMEA.

The challenge

Highlights show what happened. The competitive audience also wants to know why, and the league needed a format that fed that appetite between broadcasts.

My role

I ran Run It Back as part of the league's always-on slate: in-house at Riot Games in 2023, and at Edelman in 2024.

Strategic approach

A format for the competitive fan:

  1. Pick the momentStart from plays fans are already talking about.
  2. Put the player in the chairPlay analysis with the player, not just over the top of them.
  3. Cover both circuitsEpisodes with VCT EMEA and VCT Game Changers EMEA players.

What I delivered

  1. Five episodes across the 2023 and 2024 slates
  2. Episodes with VCT EMEA and Game Changers EMEA players
