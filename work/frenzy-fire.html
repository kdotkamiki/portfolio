Quiz series

Frenzy Fire

Riot Games · In-house, then at Edelman

Client
Riot Games
Employer
Riot Games, then Edelman
Year
2023–24
Platforms
YouTube, X, Instagram, TikTok

Overview

A quick-fire quiz series that tests VCT EMEA pros on their own game and league, and became one of the league's most popular formats.

The challenge

The league needed always-on content between broadcasts that showed players' personalities, was efficient to produce, and worked in clips across platforms.

My role

Frenzy Fire was part of the VCT EMEA content slate I led at Riot Games in 2023: I owned the format's place in the content strategy, planned the question sets and player line-ups with production, and managed publishing and cut-downs across platforms.

At Edelman in 2024, I carried the series forward, evolving the format and tracking its year-on-year growth.

Strategic approach

An always-on franchise engineered for repeatability and reach:

  1. Keep the format simpleQuick-fire questions, a scoreboard and players who really want to win.
  2. Make it work with any playerThe same format scales across the whole league, every week.
  3. Build for clippingEvery round is a standalone moment for Shorts, Reels, TikTok and X.
  4. Evolve in season twoNew mechanics in 2024, with players guessing which pro said a given quote.

Creative idea

Quick-fire questions, bold graphics, a scoreboard and players who really want to win.

What I delivered

  1. Seven-episode 2023 series
  2. 2024 series, including He Said That?
