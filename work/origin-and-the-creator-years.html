Overview

Twelve years making my own content, from gaming montages to full-time streaming. It's where I learned what makes people watch, share and come back.

The challenge

As an independent creator there's no media budget and no brand to borrow attention from. Every view has to be earned through the work itself, and the community has to want to come back.

My role

Creator, producer, editor and community lead, all at once: concept, capture, edit, publishing, scheduling and the whole content pipeline, plus community building across Discord, YouTube and X and brand partnerships with OMEN by HP, Intel and Snickers. I also competed at events including TwitchCon and EGX.

Strategic approach

Audience growth without a media budget:

  1. Make every piece an eventMontages edited to be watched, rewatched and shared.
  2. Build a home for the communityDiscord, YouTube and X as places people come back to, not just channels.
  3. Show up consistently380 streams and 2,187 hours live in 2018 alone.
  4. Grow year on yearUse the data to see what's working and do more of it.

Creative idea

Origin, a Call of Duty montage that was groundbreaking for its time and got the attention of notable creators including KSI.

What I delivered

  1. Origin and other gaming montages
  2. Live streaming: 380 streams in 2018
  3. Community across Discord, YouTube and X
  4. Brand relationships with OMEN by HP, Intel and Snickers
