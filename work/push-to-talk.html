08

Sponsored series

Push to Talk × Logitech G

Riot Games · Logitech G · In-house, then at Edelman

Client
Riot Games · Logitech G
Employer
Riot Games, then Edelman
Year
2023–24
Platforms
YouTube, X

Overview

A sponsored series I created with Logitech G: mic-check content that lets fans hear players talk in and out of the game as they prepare, set up and compete.

The challenge

Sponsor content in esports is easy to skip. Logitech needed an integration that made sense for a headset and microphone brand and that fans would actually want to watch.

My role

I created Push to Talk and owned it end to end: the format, the partnership pitch and working relationship with Logitech, comms capture and edit direction, publishing, and performance reporting to the partner. I launched it at Riot Games in 2023 and redesigned the format, structure and layout at Edelman in 2024.

Strategic approach

Branded content where the product is the point of view:

  1. Start from the product truthLogitech makes the headsets and microphones players use, so the content should be about what you hear through them.
  2. Make the integration the formatMic-check content can't exist without the product, so the brand never feels bolted on.
  3. Give fans something newComms, calls and banter they can't get from the broadcast alone.
  4. Amplify with paidLogitech sponsored the series and put paid support behind it.
  5. Iterate the formatA redesigned structure and layout in year two, built on what the 2023 data told us.

Creative idea

Push to Talk: a mic-check format. Player comms, banter and calls during matches, cut together so fans hear the game the way the players do.

What I delivered

  1. Seven-episode series in 2023
  2. A redesigned format, structure and layout in 2024
  3. Paid support in partnership with Logitech
