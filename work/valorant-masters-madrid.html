05

International event hype

VALORANT Masters Madrid

Riot Games · At Edelman

Client
Riot Games
Employer
Edelman
Year
2024
Platforms
YouTube, X, Instagram, TikTok

Overview

Creative direction for the hype campaign around VALORANT Masters Madrid 2024, the first international event of the season, as it arrived in a new host city.

The challenge

International events compete with every other moment in the esports calendar. The job was to generate hype for Madrid: make the event feel unmissable to existing fans and give the host city a role in the story.

My role

As Social & Content Lead at Edelman on the Riot Games account, I owned the creative and content pipeline for VALORANT Esports across EMEA: shaping briefs, setting creative direction, steering production partners and reporting performance back to senior stakeholders across nine markets.

On Masters Madrid my role was creative direction: setting the creative vision, briefing and guiding the production team, reviewing cuts against the brief and making sure the finished film was built to perform on social.

Strategic approach

Hype built on a sense of place:

  1. Start from the cityMake Madrid itself a character, not just a backdrop for logos and dates.
  2. Bridge the seasonConnect the EMEA league to the international stage so fans feel the jump in stakes.
  3. Build to the revealPace the film towards the Masters Madrid moment and the event branding.
  4. Distribute nativelyCut and sequence the film for each platform in the run-up to the event.

Creative idea

Hello, Madrid: a journey into the host city that lands on the Masters Madrid reveal, announcing that VALORANT's international stage has arrived.

What I delivered

  1. Creative direction on the Hello Madrid hype film
  2. Social rollout in the run-up to the event
