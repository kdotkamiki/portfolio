14

Experiential

Gekko's Bubble Tea Truck & the Fan Wall

Riot Games · In-house

Client
Riot Games
Employer
Riot Games (in-house)
Year
2023
Platforms
IRL, X, Instagram

Overview

Two in-person moments for VALORANT fans in Berlin: Gekko's bubble tea truck, part of a wider out-of-home campaign, and a signed wall that made the fans part of the league's history.

The challenge

The studio in Berlin gave the league a physical home. The opportunity was to give fans things to do there, and around the city, that they'd share and remember.

My role

As Social Media Strategist, EMEA at Riot Games, I owned the social and creative strategy for VALORANT Esports in the region, connecting audience insight with global brand objectives and taking campaigns from brief to launch to measurement.

The Gekko bubble tea truck was part of a larger out-of-home campaign, and I managed the social side of it end to end: the content plan, capture on site, community engagement and reporting on sentiment and reach. The fan wall was my idea, planned from the start as both a social moment and a piece of VCT EMEA history.

Strategic approach

Experiential, designed to be shared:

  1. IRL firstBuild experiences people genuinely want to be part of.
  2. Social by designMake every activation photographable and give fans a reason to post it.
  3. Extend the reachTurn the truck's run around Berlin and its time in the studio into social content for fans who weren't there.
  4. Leave something behindA fan wall that lives on after the event as part of the league's story.

Creative idea

Gekko's bubble tea truck

A VALORANT-branded bubble tea truck themed on the agent Gekko, for bubble tea fans and VALORANT fans alike, as part of a wider 2023 out-of-home campaign. It was parked near Alexanderplatz in Berlin for three weekends only, then came into the studio, where visitors used the tokens they got on entry to redeem a free drink.

The fan wall

A VCT EMEA-branded wall at the Coliseum where fans could sign their names and leave their mark on the league's history.

What I delivered

  1. Social campaign for the Gekko truck, end to end
  2. Fan wall concept and activation
  3. Social content from both

Results

2,000+

Free bubble teas served

The truck was measured on sentiment and reach. It was one of the most popular activations of the year, and fans asked for it to come back.
