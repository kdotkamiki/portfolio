Tentpole campaign films

VCT EMEA 2024: Season Teasers

Riot Games · At Edelman

Client
Riot Games
Employer
Edelman
Year
2024–25
Platforms
YouTube, X, Instagram, TikTok

Overview

The films that opened, restarted and closed VALORANT's EMEA league in its biggest year so far, each built around what was new at that point in the season.

The challenge

2024 brought a new Kickoff tournament, a new team promoted through Ascension, and a season split into stages. Each stage needed its own reason to tune in, without repeating the same hype formula the audience had already seen.

My role

As Social & Content Lead at Edelman on the Riot Games account, I owned the creative and content pipeline for VALORANT Esports across EMEA: shaping briefs, setting creative direction, steering production partners and reporting performance back to senior stakeholders across nine markets.

On the season teasers I led creative development and campaign planning: writing and shaping the creative briefs, working with video studio partners from concept to final cut, aligning Riot's product, brand and game teams, and building the cross-platform rollout. On the 2025 Kickoff teaser, my role was creative input and social campaign planning.

Strategic approach

A tentpole system that makes every chapter of the season feel like an event:

  1. Find the story of the momentKickoff is about change, Stage 2 about a new look and new stakes, the Grand Finals about legacy.
  2. Give each chapter a distinct creative worldNew visual language and tone each time, so the audience never feels they've seen it before.
  3. Build on the 2024 brandUse the league's new colours and imagery as the connective tissue across every film.
  4. Cut natively for every platformBespoke edits for YouTube, X, Instagram and TikTok rather than one asset shared everywhere.
  5. Launch, read, iterateTrack reach, engagement and sentiment per teaser and carry the learnings into the next chapter.
